## Statement Regarding the Repositories Formerly Hosted Under This Account (taken offline August 6, 2026)

**1. The AO3 archive.** This repository, active from 2021 to December 19, 2023, was a
fan-made preservation archive: it collected and stored Valvert (Javert/Valjean) fanworks
from AO3, organized by posting date, with a metadata catalog. Its README, written in 2023,
stated that it was non-commercial, that all works remain the property of their creators,
that it was made in line with AO3's stance on fanwork protection and AI training sets, and
that any author's removal request would be honored promptly. That contemporaneous framing
reflects what the project was: an attempt at preservation, not a training corpus. The
original README is reproduced in full below; the 2023 commit timestamps are preserved and
available to the OTW on request.

**Policy timeline.** During the repository's active period, the OTW's public position —
official announcement of May 13, 2023 (archiveofourown.org/admin_posts/25888) — was that
no policy existed against responsible data collection, with fan backups of works named
among the examples; countermeasures then in place targeted abusive large-scale collection
for AI training. The explicit prohibition on scraping entered the Terms of Service in the
2024 update (see the official guide, archiveofourown.org/works/58283857, and the current
ToS at archiveofourown.org/tos), scoped to commercial scraping including AI-dataset
creation — after this repository's final update.

**What the timeline and the intent do not excuse.** Hosting complete copies of other
creators' works in a public repository is republication. A removal-on-request mechanism is
not a substitute for consent, and those rights were never mine to administer. For this I
apologize without reservation to every author whose work was included, individually and
collectively.

**2. The site-outage mirror.** A separate repository was created as a temporary mirror of
works from another fan site during a maintenance outage. Its README
stated it was for access during the outage only, and asked readers to register on the
original site to support the authors there. It should have been deleted the moment the
site recovered; through my failure to maintain this account, it was instead left public
for years. Temporary intent and good intentions do not change the substance: where those
works are published was their authors' decision to make, not mine. That repository has
been deleted entirely, and I am reaching out to the affected author directly to apologize.

**Current status.** The AO3 archive has been taken offline in full; a private copy is
retained solely for cooperating with any OTW inquiry and will be permanently deleted once
the matter is resolved. The outage mirror has been removed entirely.
Aside from the two repositories described above, no repository under this account has ever contained works by other creators. The remaining repositories are personal storage for my own writing and files, and were never intended as public archives.


Should the OTW contact me regarding this matter, I will provide this same account of
events and cooperate fully.

## Appendix: Original README of the AO3 archive (published 2023; contact email redacted)

Welcome to the Valvert_AO3_Archive, a dedicated repository for all Valvert
(Javert/Valjean) works within the Les Misérables fandom on Archive of Our Own (AO3).

**Overview.** This archive is a collection of fanworks centered around the Valvert
(Javert/Valjean) pairing from the Les Misérables fandom. It is intended as a resource for
fans to explore and enjoy the diversity and creativity within this specific niche of the
fandom. Contents are organized based on the first posting date.

**Features.**
Comprehensive CSV File: The archive includes a CSV file detailing meta information (title,
author, rating, etc.) for each work. This serves as a quick reference guide for exploring
the collection.
Respect for Authorship: While this collection is a labor of love for fans by fans, it is
important to acknowledge and respect the rights of the original creators. This archive is
non-commercial and purely for fan interest.
Ethical Considerations and Removal Requests: This archive adheres to ethical
considerations in fanwork usage in line with AO3's stance on fanwork protection and their
position regarding AI training sets. If your work is included in this archive and you wish
to have it removed, please reach out to me directly. I am committed to respecting the
wishes of the original creators and will quickly address any removal requests.

**Contact.** For removal requests, inquiries, or further information, please get in touch
with [email redacted].

**Disclaimer.** All fanworks in this archive are the property of their respective
creators. The Valvert_AO3_Archive does not claim ownership of any works presented herein.
This project is a fan-made effort to catalog and celebrate Valvert fanworks from the Les
Misérables fandom. It operates under the principles of respect, admiration, and
non-commercial sharing within the fan community.
