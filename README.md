## 关于已删除的存档项目的说明与致歉

2021 年至 2023 年 12 月，我在 GitHub 上运营过一个 AO3 Valvert 相关作品的存档项目，分别于 2021 年和 2023 年上传更新。存档范围为当时在 AO3 上公开可见的作品，其中包含未经授权、以明文形式再发布的他人作品；项目链接曾长期在我的仓库公开可见。

未经授权再发布他人的作品是错误的。这一点不取决于当年的平台条款，不取决于项目的性质或初衷，也不取决于有多少人看到。作品的去向应当由作者本人决定，而我替作者做了这个决定。对此我向所有作品曾出现在该存档中的作者道歉。

公开存档及相关仓库已全部删除，不会再以任何形式发布。此事已在 OTW 处，我将全力配合他们的任何核查与处理，并以他们的沟通为准。作品曾出现在存档中的作者如需向我提出任何要求，可通过本页留言或 [ChristyKim0606@gmail.com](mailto:ChristyKim0606@gmail.com) 联系我，我会回应。

## Statement and Apology Regarding a Deleted Archive Project

From 2021 to December 2023, I ran a GitHub archive of Valvert works from AO3, with updates uploaded in 2021 and 2023. The archive covered works that were publicly visible on AO3 at the time, and included other authors' works republished in plain text without authorization. A link to the project was publicly visible on my profile for an extended period.

Republishing others' works without permission was wrong. This does not depend on the platform's terms of service at the time, on the nature or intent of the project, or on how many people saw it. Authors should decide where their work goes, and I made that decision for them. I apologize to every author whose work appeared in that archive.

The public archive and all related repositories have been permanently deleted and will not be republished in any form. This matter is with the OTW, and I will fully cooperate with any review or action they undertake, and will follow their communication on next steps. Authors whose works appeared in the archive are welcome to leave a comment on this page or contact me at [ChristyKim0606@gmail.com](mailto:ChristyKim0606@gmail.com) with any requests, and I will respond.
